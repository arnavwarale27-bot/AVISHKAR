## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional, and mathematically rigorous environment for the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India. Designed specifically for the National Airfare Price Index (APIx India), the interface reflects sovereign data integrity, fiscal exactitude, and high-density econometric transparency. It firmly rejects consumer travel metaphors (no flight paths, booking engines, or destination imagery) in favor of high-throughput econometric telemetry, algorithmic confidence ratings, methodology proofs, and regulatory auditing matrices.

The aesthetic philosophy aligns with **Institutional High-Density Modernism**:
- **Absolute Precision**: Data density is prioritized over decorative negative space. Information arrays, formula breakdowns, and timeseries matrices present complete statistical contexts without gating data behind excessive disclosures.
- **Sovereign Authority**: A disciplined architectural structure built on deep government slate blues, crisp field backgrounds, and restrained Indian saffron accents for critical index notifications and sovereign markers.
- **Auditable Clarity**: Every metric displays its underlying provenance, sampling depth, volatility bounds, and Laspeyres/Jevons index formula attribution directly within its operational context.

## Colors

The palette delivers maximum contrast ratio adherence (WCAG AAA for all tabular data and metrics) while maintaining sovereign institutional gravity:

- **Primary & Structural Ink (`#0B192C`, `#1E3E62`)**: Deep Bharat Navy and Sovereign Slate Blue. Serves as top-level institutional headers, primary data series, dominant typography, and authoritative status structures.
- **National Signal Amber/Saffron (`#D97706`, `#EA580C`, `#F59E0B`)**: Restricted strictly to official index benchmarks, national baseline indicators, sovereign audit badges, and calibration flags. Never used for generic warning notifications.
- **Surfaces & Tonal Dividers (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`)**: Clean slate paper surfaces. Neutral 50 (`#F8FAFC`) anchors page backgrounds to eliminate optical glare under continuous analytical review, while pure white (`#FFFFFF`) isolates active computation cards and mathematical matrices.
- **Econometric Validation Green (`#059669`, `#10B981`)**: Used exclusively for verified sample sets, deflationary price indices, stable algorithmic thresholds, and healthy pipeline pipelines.
- **Statistical Deviation Crimson (`#DC2626`, `#B91C1C`)**: Indicates statistical anomalies, outlier exclusions, route price surges crossing standard deviations, and API ingestion telemetry alerts.

## Typography

The typography architecture bridges institutional administrative prose with precise computational reporting.

- **Inter**: Drives the user interface, metadata hierarchy, tables, analytical reports, and administrative narratives. Its neutral geometry and tall x-height guarantee legibility across multi-tiered tabular grids.
- **JetBrains Mono**: Deployed for all mathematical indices, statistical series values, currency markers (INR), route codes (OD pairs e.g., DEL-BLR), sample sizes ($N$), confidence intervals, and formula displays.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics**: Monospaced tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced across all numeric outputs to prevent visual jitter in real-time streaming calculation matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a compressed 12-column analytical grid optimized for dense statistical dashboards and multi-variant econometric models.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12 columns with standard 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas margins on desktop environments (1440px+). On mobile viewports (<768px), it collapses to a single or dual-column stacked flow with 8px gutters and 12px margins.
- **Density Scaling**: Standard UI components adopt dense spacing defaults (`space-sm` at 6px, `space-md` at 12px) to maximize screen real estate, allowing simultaneous visibility of index trends, route-level dispersion buckets, and mathematical weighting factors without continuous viewport scrolling.
- **Panels & Dividers**: Visual grouping relies heavily on precise 1px hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) rather than large whitespace separations, keeping related statistical contexts physically unified.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines & Tonal Grounding** rather than dramatic blur shadows, reinforcing a sober, authoritative, audit-ready atmosphere.

- **Flat Precision Grounding**: Cards and computation units do not float; they sit grounded within the interface grid. Primary panels use a crisp 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1` or `#E2E8F0`) over an absolute white (`#FFFFFF`) surface.
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50).
- **Layer 1 (Analytical Surfaces & Matrices)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E2E8F0`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Parameter Selectors)**: `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#94A3B8`) accompanied by a tight, disciplined institutional shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modal Audit Logs & Sovereign Seals)**: `#FFFFFF` with a hairline navy tint border (`#1E3E62`) and a structured backdrop barrier (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The shape system is set to **Soft (`1`)** with a maximum operational radius of 4px (`0.25rem`) across standard cards, table shells, and interactive controls, shifting to 2px (`0.125rem`) for sub-badges and code blocks.

- **Geometry**: Sharp corners slightly softened to 4px maintain an institutional, ledger-like precision while eliminating harsh optical apexes.
- **Data Cells & Badges**: Data chips, status indicators, and route-pair identifiers use strict 2px to 3px radius caps. Fully rounded pills (border-radius 9999px) are prohibited to avoid the casual visual language of consumer travel applications.

## Components

### 1. Statistical Index Cards (KPI Blocks)
- Container: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 4px radius, 12px inner padding (`space-md`).
- Header: 11px uppercase label (`label-mono`, `#64748B`) followed by the official Laspeyres/Fisher methodology badge.
- Metric: 28px `JetBrains Mono` (`#0B192C`), paired with directional dispersion tag:
  - Green (`#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`) for downward index movement / deflationary signal.
  - Red (`#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626`) for upward index movement / inflationary spike.
- Footnote: Standard sample volume indicator ($N = 1,420,891$) and baseline deviation marker ($Base = 100$).

### 2. Mathematical Equation & Methodology Presentation Blocks
- Surface: `#F1F5F9`, bordered by `#CBD5E1` with a left structural stroke of 3px in `#1E3E62`.
- Content: Formal formula display (e.g., $P_L = \frac{\sum (p_{t} \cdot q_{0})}{\sum (p_{0} \cdot q_{0})}$) rendered with high-contrast glyphs, accompanied by active weighting weights ($W_i$) displayed in JetBrains Mono.

### 3. Pipeline Flow & Data Provenance Telemetry
- Pipeline Node: Monospaced step indicators (`SCRAPE_INGEST` → `CLEAN_OUTLIER` → `PAASCHE_AGGREGATE` → `INDEX_PUBLISH`).
- Node status marked with 6px geometric indicators: Emerald for nominal latency (<120ms), Saffron for ingest throttle, Crimson for schema breach.

### 4. High-Density Tabular Grids
- Header: Height 32px, background `#0B192C`, text `#F8FAFC` (11px JetBrains Mono, uppercase).
- Rows: Height 36px, alternating row striping (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`), bottom border `#E2E8F0`. Numeric columns right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Interactive Row Select: Border highlight `#1E3E62` with light slate tint (`#F1F5F9`).

### 5. Sovereign Identification & Badges
- MoSPI Validation Ribbon: Embedded at the dashboard header with sovereign tri-color micro-bar (2px baseline: `#FF9933`, `#FFFFFF`, `#138808`), followed by strict institutional identifier: "MINISTRY OF STATISTICS & PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATION — CENTRAL DATA REPOSITORY".
- Status Badges: 2px border radius, 10px uppercase mono font, padding 2px 6px. Saffron (`#FEF3C7` / `#B45309`), Emerald (`#D1FAE5` / `#047857`), Navy (`#E2E8F0` / `#0B192C`).

### 6. Interactive Filter & Parameter Bar
- Consolidated 32px high inline controls: Origin-Destination pair selector, Date Horizon picker (T-1 to T-365), Airline Seat-Class Weighting multiplier toggle.
- Primary Buttons: Solid `#0B192C`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#1E3E62`, no drop shadow.
- Secondary Actions: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, hover `#F8FAFC`.
