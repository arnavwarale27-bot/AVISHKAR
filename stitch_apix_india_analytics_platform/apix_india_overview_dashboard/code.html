High-Frequency Price Indicator (HFPI) for CPI Transport Sub-Group

The APIx is an experimental econometric initiative designed by the National Statistical Office (NSO) to assess the statistical validity of alternative web-scraped microdata for official Consumer Price Index (CPI) augmentation. By monitoring 140,000+ daily booking points across the 48 core domestic air travel corridors, APIx computes a high-fidelity continuous price trajectory that leads official survey bulletins by up to 24 days.
